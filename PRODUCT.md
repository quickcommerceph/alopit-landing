## Product

openly is a talent network where people build personal, portfolio-first profiles and earn when a hiring team makes a qualified paid open. Teams browse structured previews for free, then spend credits to unlock contact details and start a direct conversation.

The first acquisition surface has two jobs:

1. Convince talent that their profile is a living asset, not another unpaid application.
2. Convince hiring teams that pay-per-intent is a better signal than buying résumé volume.

## Users

**Talent**: designers, builders, strategists, makers, and independent professionals who want a richer profile than a CV and more control over who contacts them.

**Teams**: founders, hiring managers, and recruiters who want to discover work and point of view before committing to a call.

## Business model

- Talent profiles are free to create and keep live.
- Teams search and preview profiles for free.
- Teams buy credits to open contact details and send a direct connection.
- A qualified paid open credits the talent wallet; the exact amount, fraud controls, refund rules, and payout thresholds are product decisions still to validate.

## Product principles

- Personal profiles over flattened résumés.
- Privacy by default: identity and contact details stay behind the paid open.
- Pay for intent, not impressions: deduplicate opens and detect abuse before paying out.
- Make the work legible: show context, links, selected work, availability, and point of view.
- Be warm and particular, not corporate or gamified.

## Accessibility

Use semantic markup, keyboard-reachable controls, sufficient contrast, responsive layouts, useful alt text, and reduced-motion alternatives for animation.
