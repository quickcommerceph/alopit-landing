# Design System: openly

## Creative direction

Physical scene: a jobseeker checks their living profile on a late commute while a small hiring team scans a contact sheet of people who have something specific to say.

The visual world is a scrapbook-meets-studio dashboard: warm paper, electric blue, signal orange, acid lime, and black ink. Profiles feel like places with a point of view, while the hiring surface feels deliberate and legible. The palette stays committed and playful without drifting into generic SaaS gradients or corporate blue.

Typography uses Space Grotesk for the voice of the brand, Manrope for readable interface copy, and DM Mono for compact metadata. Large type is tight but not cramped; text wraps are balanced and body copy stays readable.

## Core patterns

- Flat paper sections alternate with black, lilac, blue, and coral bands.
- Use orange for action and attention, blue for trust and discovery, lime for positive state and earnings.
- Use small, square-ish content panels for profile artifacts; use pills only for compact status or navigation controls.
- Product previews are made from real interface structures, not abstract gradient blobs.
- Motion is limited to gentle upward reveals and small hover lifts; reduced motion disables movement.

## Messaging

- Talent: “Your profile shouldn’t work for free.”
- Teams: “Stop buying résumés. Find signals.”
- Product promise: “The talent network with a return.”
- Use “qualified open” rather than implying every raw page view is paid.
